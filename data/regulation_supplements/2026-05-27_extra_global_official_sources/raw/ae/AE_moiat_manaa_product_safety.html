Manaa System

Manaa is an innovative system that ensures the safety and quality of products, Manaa is product recall management system. It is accessible to society, individuals, government departments, private sectors, suppliers and others, the system also allows the consumer to report injuries and accidents caused by using of non-complying products, as well as to report the finding of a recalled or non-complying products on the market.
