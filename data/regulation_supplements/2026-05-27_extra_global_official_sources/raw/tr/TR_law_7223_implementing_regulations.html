Implementing Regulations

Implementing Regulations of the Law No. 7223 on Product Safety and Technical Regulations is provided below:

The Regulation on General Product Safety was published in the Official Gazette dated 11/03/2021 and numbered 31420 and entered into force on 12/03/2021 simultaneously with the Law no. 7223.

                    https://www.resmigazete.gov.tr/eskiler/2021/03/20210311-18.htm

The Regulation on Mutual Recognition in the Non-Harmonized Area came into force by being published in the Official Gazette dated 12/03/2021 and numbered 31421.

                    https://www.resmigazete.gov.tr/eskiler/2021/03/20210312-3.htm

CE Marking Regulation was published in the Official Gazette dated 27/05/2021 and numbered 31493, and entered into force.

                   https://www.resmigazete.gov.tr/eskiler/2021/05/20210527-2.pdf

 Conformity Assessment Bodies and Notified Bodies Regulation was published in the Official Gazette dated 27/05/2021 and numbered 31493, and entered into force.

                   https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/MevzuatMetin/21.5.4022.pdf

Conformity Assessment Methods Regulation was published in the Official Gazette dated 27/05/2021 and numbered 31493, and entered into force.

                   https://www.resmigazete.gov.tr/eskiler/2021/05/20210527-5.htm

Presidential Decision No. 4269 on the Enforcement of The Framework Regulation on Market Surveillance and Inspection of Products was published in the Official Gazette No. 31537 on 10 July 2021.

                  https://www.resmigazete.gov.tr/eskiler/2021/07/20210710-21.pdf

Regulation on the Notification of Technical Regulations of Standards between Türkiye and the European Union came into force by being published in the Official Gazette dated  3 April 2021 and numbered 24715.

https://www.resmigazete.gov.tr/eskiler/2002/04/20020403.htm#4

Regulation on Market Surveillance of Products Placed on the Market Through Means of Distance Communication was published in the Official Gazette dated 30 October 2024 and numbered 32707 and came into force on 1 April 2025.

 https://www.resmigazete.gov.tr/eskiler/2024/10/20241030-2.htm
